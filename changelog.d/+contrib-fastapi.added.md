`even_simpler_salesforce.contrib.fastapi.SalesforceIntegration` (`[fastapi]` extra): creates and closes one `AsyncSalesforce` in the app lifespan, gives routes the app-wide client (`admin`) or a per-user child (`user`, through `auth_for=`) as dependencies, reports the `esf.capture()` roll-up of each request to `on_summary=`, and with `error_handlers=True` maps typed errors to JSON responses (401, 422, 404, 400 for an invalid cursor, 503 with `Retry-After`, 502).
